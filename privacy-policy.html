Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

BoomRocks ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile arcade game developed by Kinex Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use BoomRocks.

1. Information We Collect

BoomRocks does not directly collect personal information. However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect certain data:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency: On iOS 14.5+, we request your permission before any tracking occurs. You can change this at any time in your device Settings.

2. How We Use Information

Any data collected through third-party services is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Improve user experience

3. Data Storage

Game progress (scores, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not transmitted to any server.

4. In-App Purchases

BoomRocks offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") that disables all advertisements. Purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store; we do not receive or store your payment information. Apple may retain purchase records under its own privacy policy. You can restore previous purchases at any time from the in-app Settings screen.

5. Third-Party Services

BoomRocks uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying ads (Privacy Policy)

6. Children's Privacy

BoomRocks is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's privacy settings.
  • You can deny tracking when the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
