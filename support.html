Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my progress?

Your game progress is automatically saved on your device. Scores, coins, guns, and upgrades are all saved in real time. Note: uninstalling the app will delete your progress.

Why am I seeing ads?

BoomRocks is a free-to-play game supported by advertisements. You will see banner ads on menu screens and occasional interstitial ads between games. Rewarded ads are always optional and give you bonus coins.

How does the 2x Coins feature work?

After each game, you can tap the gold "2x Coins" button to watch a short video ad. After watching the full ad, you'll receive double the coins you earned that game as a bonus.

How do I unlock new guns?

Visit the Shop from the main menu. You can browse all available guns and purchase them with coins earned from gameplay. Each gun has unique stats and special abilities.

What is the Daily Gift?

Visit the main menu every day to claim your daily gift. The rewards follow a 7-day cycle with increasing coin bonuses. Come back every day to keep your streak going!

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap the "Remove Ads" card to make a one-time purchase that permanently removes all banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. The purchase is non-consumable and tied to your Apple ID.

I bought Remove Ads on another device — how do I get it here?

Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, your Remove Ads purchase will be restored at no additional cost.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and tap "Reset Progress". This will reset all coins, scores, guns, and upgrades. This action cannot be undone.
